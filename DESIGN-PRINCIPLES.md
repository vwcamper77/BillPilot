# Design principles

- The first screen answers exactly four things: what's in your account now, bills due before you're paid, days to stretch it, and what's clear to spend. Everything else is drill-down or a tool.
- One answer at the top; arithmetic and detail one tap away.
- Whole pounds on display; full precision in calculations.
- "Clear to spend" is the term everywhere — never "safe to spend", with no synonyms such as "free cash" or "allowance".
- Nothing is removed, only repositioned.
